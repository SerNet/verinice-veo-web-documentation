# Working in the Grundschutz++ Domain

In the **Grundschutz++** (GS++) domain, you map the BSI's Grundschutz++ methodology to your institution. Grundschutz++ is the further development of IT-Grundschutz: the modules of the IT-Grundschutz Compendium are replaced by a machine-readable requirements catalog from the BSI's state-of-the-art library. From this catalog, you compile an individual ***Anforderungspaket*** (requirements package) for your *Informationsverbund* (information domain) using ***Praktiken*** (practices) and ***Zielobjektkategorien*** (target object categories).

The methodology follows a PDCA cycle with five process steps. This manual is structured along these steps:

- Get to know the [Grundschutz++ methodology](./gs-pp-methodology) and the differences from IT-Grundschutz.
- Get to know the [Grundschutz++ domain](./domain-gs-pp-description) and the [requirements catalog](./catalog).
- Get an overview of a fully modeled example with the [profile of the domain](./gs-pp-profiles).
- **Process step 1 – *Erhebung und Planung* (survey and planning):** Define the institution, *Anwendungsbereich* (scope of application) and business processes ([survey and planning](./planning)) and carry out the [*Schutzbedarfsfeststellung* (protection need assessment)](./protection-needs).
- **Process step 2 – *Anforderungsanalyse* (requirements analysis):** [Delimit the *Informationsverbund* and record the assets](./structure-analysis), assign them to [*Zielobjektkategorien*](./target-object-categories) and [compile the *Anforderungspaket*](./modelling).
- **Process step 3 – *Realisierung* (realization):** Determine the [implementation status and plan the implementation](./implementation).
- Where required, carry out the [*Risikobetrachtung* (risk consideration)](./risk-analysis).
- **Process steps 4 and 5 – *Überwachung* (monitoring) and *kontinuierliche Verbesserung* (continuous improvement):** Keep your ISMS up to date with [monitoring and improvement](./monitoring-improvement).

::: info Status of the methodology
Grundschutz++ is in its introduction and trial phase. This manual is based on the [*Leitfaden* (guide) to the Grundschutz++ methodology](https://www.bsi.bund.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/BSI/Grundschutz/sonstiges/Methodik_Grundschutz_PlusPlus.html) (March 2026) and the *Pilotierungsfassung* (pilot version) of the methodology in version 0.2 (31.07.2026), which incorporates the feedback from the pilot phase. Where the two versions differ, the manual describes the state of the *Pilotierungsfassung* and points out the earlier state.
:::
